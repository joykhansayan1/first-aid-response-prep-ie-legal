Walk into your First Aid Response course already knowing the theory. Original revision material written from the published PHECC First Aid Response Clinical Practice Guidelines index and the Resuscitation Council UK 2025 guidelines, with a citation on every single explanation.

In a real emergency, call 112 or 999

Both numbers are free from any phone in Ireland, and 112 works throughout the European Union. This app is theory revision. It is not medical advice, it is not a diagnosis, and it is not a substitute for the emergency services.

This app does not certify anyone

A PHECC First Aid Response certificate is issued only after you attend a course in person with a PHECC-recognised institution, taught by a certified FAR instructor, and pass the assessment — which is skills-based, with a short multiple-choice paper alongside it. PHECC publishes that a FAR course takes no less than 18 teaching contact hours and that the certificate expires after two years, at which point the holder may recertify.

No app can replace that. This one exists to make the theory easy before you get there — and to keep it fresh afterwards.

Where the content comes from

Every question, rationale, scene, sequence, outline and glossary entry is original material written for this app. Nothing is copied from PHECC course material, from any recognised institution's manuals or slides, from any book, or from any other app.

Clinical steps, figures and sequences are written from publicly published material:

  • the PHECC First Aid Response Clinical Practice Guidelines 2026 index and the 2026 (8th) edition of the Irish Clinical Practice Guidelines;
  • PHECC's published education and training standards;
  • the Resuscitation Council UK 2025 Resuscitation Guidelines and the ILCOR 2025 international consensus on first aid science;
  • published Irish statutory, emergency-service and patient-organisation material, including the emergency call service, the National Poisons Information Centre, the Health and Safety Authority, the Irish Heart Foundation, the Asthma Society of Ireland, Diabetes Ireland, Epilepsy Ireland and Water Safety Ireland.

Every one of these is listed, with a tappable link, in Sources & Citations inside the app — reachable from every tab. Guidelines change; always confirm the current wording with the primary source, and work only within the level you are trained and certified to.
