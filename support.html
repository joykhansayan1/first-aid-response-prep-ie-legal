Purchases

How do I restore a purchase?

On the purchase screen, tap Restore Purchases. If you have already bought and are inside the app, go to Home → gear icon (top right) → Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases. That button is always there, before and after purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

I paid but the app still shows the purchase screen

  1. Check you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought with.
  2. Tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen.
  3. Make sure you have a network connection — StoreKit needs one to verify.
  4. Force-quit the app and relaunch it; entitlement is re-checked on every launch and whenever the app becomes active.
  5. If it still fails, email us with the date of purchase and we will help.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In the app: Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription. Or on your device: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Certification

Does this app certify me?

No. A PHECC First Aid Response certificate is issued only after you attend a course in person with a PHECC-recognised institution, taught by a certified FAR instructor, and pass the assessment — which is skills-based, with a short multiple-choice paper alongside it. PHECC publishes that a FAR course takes no less than 18 teaching contact hours. This app is theory revision that makes that day easier. It cannot replace it.

How long is a FAR certificate valid?

PHECC publishes that the joint PHECC/recognised-institution First Aid Response certificate expires after two years, at which point the holder may recertify. Nothing renews automatically. You can set your expiry date in Settings → Your course to get a countdown.

How do I find a recognised institution?

Only PHECC-recognised institutions may deliver a FAR course and issue the certificate. Check a provider's recognition on the PHECC website before you book or pay for anything.

Using the app

Where do the answers come from?

Every explanation names the published source it was written from and links to it. Tap Sources & Citations — it is reachable from every tab — for the full list. Content is written from the published PHECC First Aid Response Clinical Practice Guidelines index and education standards, the Resuscitation Council UK 2025 guidelines, the ILCOR 2025 consensus, and published Irish statutory, emergency-service and patient-organisation material.

I think a question is wrong

Please tell us. Email dmjks120@gmail.com with the question text and, if you can, the source you think is correct. Content corrections are taken seriously and are fixed in the next update. This is safety-critical material and we would far rather hear about a mistake than not.

What is the sequence drill?

An algorithm — the primary survey, adult or paediatric BLS, AED use, choking, anaphylaxis, catastrophic bleeding, FAST, METHANE, Ten Second Triage and more — with its steps shuffled. You tap them back into the right order. It uses tapping rather than dragging so it works with VoiceOver, with Switch Control, and at the largest Dynamic Type sizes. A sequence counts as mastered once you have run it start to finish with no wrong taps.

How does spaced repetition work?

Every question sits in one of five Leitner boxes. Get it right and it moves up a box and comes back later; get it wrong and it drops back to box one and comes back tomorrow. The Daily Review on the Home tab shows everything that is due.

The daily reminder is not appearing

Check Settings → Study Reminder inside the app, and then the iOS Settings app → Notifications → First Aid Response Prep IE. Notifications are the only permission this app ever requests, and it is entirely optional — the app works fully without it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every question, sequence and reference screen works with no signal. A connection is needed only to load App Store prices, complete or restore a purchase, or open a citation link in your browser.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Study Data → Reset All Progress, or Progress → Reset all progress. This deletes your stats, streaks, bookmarks, sequence attempts and test history from your device and cannot be undone.

Accessibility

The app supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type at every size including the accessibility sizes, Bold Text, Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast. Every question and every sequence can be read aloud with the speaker button. Every colour is defined separately for Light and Dark Mode so nothing loses contrast when the appearance changes. If you hit an accessibility problem, please email us — those reports get priority.
