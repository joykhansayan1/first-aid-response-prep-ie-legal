The short version

First Aid Response Prep IE does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no tracking and no server operated by us. Everything you do in the app — your answers, your progress, your sequence attempts, your bookmarks, your streak, your course date and your settings — is stored only on your own device.

1. Who this policy is from

This policy covers the iOS application First Aid Response Prep IE ("the app"), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy ("we", "us"). Contact: dmjks120@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. Specifically, the app does not collect, transmit, sell, share or store on any server:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact information;
  • your date of birth, gender or any demographic information;
  • your location, precise or coarse;
  • your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, health data or motion data — the app never requests access to any of these;
  • any health information about you or about anyone you might treat;
  • any device identifier, advertising identifier or fingerprint;
  • usage analytics, crash analytics, session recordings or behavioural data;
  • your quiz answers, scores, progress, bookmarks or study history.

We have declared "Data Not Collected" in the App Store's App Privacy section, and that declaration is accurate.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only, so that it can function between launches:

  • which questions you have answered, whether you got them right, and when — used for mastery, readiness and spaced repetition;
  • your sequence-drill attempts and best scores;
  • your bookmarked questions;
  • your practice-test history and day streak;
  • the answers you gave during onboarding: your route (first course, recertification or refresher), where you would be using first aid, your goal, your course date, your certificate expiry date if you set one, your focus modules and your daily question target;
  • your settings, including whether the optional daily reminder is on and at what time.

None of this is health information about a patient — it is your own study record. It never leaves your device and is not backed up to any server we control. If you have iCloud device backup enabled, Apple may include the app's local data in your own encrypted device backup — that is between you and Apple, and we have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time inside the app: Settings → Study Data → Reset All Progress, or Progress → Reset all progress.

4. The app is not a patient record

The app is a revision tool. It has no facility to record anything about a real patient, a real incident or a real casualty, and you should not attempt to use it as one. If you need to keep an incident record, use your organisation's own process.

5. Purchases

The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework for in-app purchases. When you buy a subscription or the lifetime unlock, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details, your Apple Account email, or your name. The app checks with Apple whether a valid purchase exists in order to unlock content, and that check happens on your device against Apple's signed transaction records.

Apple's own handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

6. Notifications

Notifications are the only system permission the app ever requests, and only if you switch the optional daily study reminder on. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device by the app. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and no push service is used. You can turn the reminder off at any time in Settings → Study Reminder, or in the iOS Settings app.

7. Network use

The app works fully offline. It makes a network connection only in two situations:

  • to communicate with the App Store through StoreKit, in order to load prices, complete a purchase or restore a purchase; and
  • if you tap an outbound citation, source or support link, which opens that address in your browser.

Every citation link points to a public website operated by a third party — for example PHECC, the Resuscitation Council UK, the National Poisons Information Centre or the Health and Safety Authority. Once you leave the app, that site's own privacy policy applies. We do not control those sites and receive nothing back from them.

8. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs of any kind: no analytics, no advertising, no attribution, no crash reporting, no A/B testing, no social login. We share no data with anyone, because we hold none.

9. Children

The app is a first aid theory revision tool intended for adults preparing for, or refreshing after, a PHECC First Aid Response course. It is not directed at children and it is not in the Kids Category. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018 you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection in respect of personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to give you, correct, or delete on our side. The data on your device is entirely under your control and can be erased by resetting your progress in the app or by deleting the app.

If you believe your data protection rights have been infringed you may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

11. Security

Because no data is transmitted or stored off-device, there is no server for us to secure and no database of user information that could be breached. On-device data is protected by iOS and by your device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date. If we ever began collecting data — which we have no plans to do — we would update this policy, update the App Store privacy declaration, and seek consent where the law requires it.

13. Contact

Email dmjks120@gmail.com with any privacy question. We aim to reply within a few working days.
