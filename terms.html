1. What this app is

First Aid Response Prep IE is an independent, unofficial study aid for people preparing for, or refreshing after, a PHECC First Aid Response (FAR) course. It contains original questions, explanations, sequence drills, casualty scenes, reference material and a glossary.

2. What this app is not

  • It is not a First Aid Response course and it is not accredited or approved by anyone.
  • It does not administer any assessment, and no result inside it counts for anything outside it.
  • It does not issue, renew or extend any certificate, and it does not register anyone with PHECC.
  • Holding, buying or completing this app satisfies no legal, contractual or workplace requirement.
  • It is not medical advice and creates no clinician–patient relationship of any kind.

A joint PHECC/recognised-institution First Aid Response certificate is issued only after attending a course in person with a PHECC-recognised institution and passing the assessment, which is skills-based with a short multiple-choice paper alongside it. PHECC publishes that the certificate expires after two years.
